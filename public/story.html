Shell→Bare Honey

1

The parcel

Alex ordered a lipstick called Shell for a friend's wedding. On the product page it looked like a soft rose.

In the mirror, on Alex, it looked grey.

A screen can't show how a colour sits on your skin. That isn't anyone's fault, and it happens every day.

2

The dead end

Most returns portals ask one question, why?, and then send you to the same place whatever you answer.

  • Alex waits days for a refund, with nothing to wear on Saturday.
  • The store loses the sale and pays for the round trip.
  • Nobody learns why Shell didn't work, so the next shopper makes the same mistake.

3

The mirror

With MirrorMatch, the same answer opens a different door: "See a better match on you before you decide? About 30 seconds."

  1. One selfie, used only for this return and deleted when it ends.
  2. YouCam's AI Facial Color Tones Analyzer measures skin, lip, eye and hair colour.
  3. Plain colour science ranks the in-stock shades by how they sit on that tone, and says why in one line.
  4. YouCam's AI Makeup Virtual Try-On shows them on Alex's own face, next to the one going back.

4

“That's me.”

Alex drags the slider: Shell on the left, Bare Honey on the right. The photo is the same; only the shade changes.

One tap: Exchange for Bare Honey. Free, ships today. In time for Saturday. The selfie is deleted, on our side and at YouCam.

And if nothing looked right? The refund was one tap away the whole time. No timers, no guilt trips.

5

Same story, any colour

A wrap dress in Camel that washed out? For clothes the goal is contrast and warmth, not a skin match. YouCam's AI Clothes Virtual Try-On shows the colourways that score better, on the shopper's own photo.

Makeup and clothes try-on, one returns flow, one decision.

6

The store learns

Every colour return is a lesson nobody was collecting. The merchant sees refunds turned into exchanges, which shades come back from which tone profiles, and where the shade range has gaps.

Live numbers only come from real sessions. Illustration data is labelled "synthetic demo data" and kept apart.

What we promise the shopper

  • The refund is always one tap away.MirrorMatch only appears for colour reasons, and it never hides the refund.
  • The photo is gone when the return ends.Kept in memory only, then deleted here and at YouCam with their task-delete API.
  • Previews are honest.Labelled "visual preview, not a guarantee", every time.
  • Decisions are plain code.Matching, stock and exchange rules are deterministic and testable. An AI model only ever rewords text.
